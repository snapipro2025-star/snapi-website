Privacy Policy

Updated September 13, 2026

About this policy

SNAPI ONE is provided by SNAPI Technologies LLC. This policy describes how information is handled when you use its nutrition tracking and accountability features.

Information saved on your device

SNAPI ONE saves the profile details you enter, including age, height, sex used for calorie estimates, activity level, eating style, goals, starting and goal weights, and dates. It also saves food entries and servings, nutrition values, your My foods list, weight records, commitment status, preferences, and archived chapters. These records support your diary, estimates, progress display, and reminders. They are stored in app files on your phone, or browser storage when you use the web version. The app does not require an account or upload these records as a personal profile to a SNAPI ONE account service.

Food searches and barcode lookups

When you search for a food or request USDA food details, the search text or food identifier is sent to the SNAPI ONE food service hosted on Cloudflare and, when needed, to USDA FoodData Central. Food responses may be cached to support future lookups. When you look up a barcode, its number is sent directly to Open Food Facts. These services receive network information such as an IP address and request details while handling connections. Do not include private personal details in food searches. Network providers may process operational or security logs under their own policies; local diary storage does not mean that food searches are anonymous.

Camera permission

Camera access is optional and is used to read food barcodes. SNAPI ONE does not save or upload camera images. You can enter barcode digits manually instead and can change camera permission in your device settings.

Advertising, analytics, and external links

This version of SNAPI ONE does not include advertising or third-party analytics tracking in its app code and does not sell your local diary or profile records. Opening links such as YouTube or a food-source website takes you to a separate service governed by that service's privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain until removed or the app's stored data is cleared. You can edit profile details in Settings and edit or remove today's food entries on Today. Delete from My foods removes only the reusable list item, not diary history. Fresh start archives earlier records; it does not erase them. To remove all local records, use your device's app-data deletion controls or delete the app and its data; on the web, clear this site's browser data. Device or browser backups may retain copies or restore data, depending on your settings. Manage those copies through your backup provider. SNAPI ONE cannot remotely erase records stored only on your device. Retention of service-provider network logs is separate from local storage.

Keeping your information private

Protect your device and its backups with appropriate access controls. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser may be able to see your records. Food lookup connections use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data handling changes. Review the date on this page for the latest version.
