Health Disclaimer

Updated September 13, 2026

Nutrition awareness and accountability

SNAPI ONE supports general nutrition awareness, food logging, and personal accountability. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not provide medical advice.

Professional guidance

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and for advice about dietary or weight changes appropriate to your circumstances. Do not use the app to replace professional care, disregard medical advice, or delay seeking care.

Estimates and food information

Calories, nutrients, portion conversions, and calorie-balance calculations are estimates. USDA and Open Food Facts information, package labels, preparation methods, and serving sizes can differ or contain errors. Check food matches and package information. The app is not a source for determining whether a food is safe for an allergy or medical condition.

Your goals and choices

Food Guidance and carb-target reminders reflect app rules and the preferences you choose; they are not clinical assessments or personalized treatment plans. No particular weight, body composition, or metabolic-health outcome is promised. Individual needs and results vary.
