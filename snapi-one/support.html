For app questions, feedback, or privacy requests, email support@snapi-technologies.com.

Include your device type, app version, and what happened. Please avoid sending medical records or other sensitive information unless needed to resolve your request.

Managing your records

Today lets you edit or remove today's food entries. Delete from My foods clears an item from the reusable list. Fresh start archives earlier records. See our Privacy Policy for information about local storage and removing app data.
